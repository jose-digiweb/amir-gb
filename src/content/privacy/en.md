## Who we are

AMIR-GB is an association (vereniging) registered in the Netherlands, KvK 42120588, at Willem van Kesselstraat 21, 4827 LJ Breda. We decide what happens with the details you give us, as Dutch and European privacy law (AVG, GDPR) asks.

## What we collect, and why

When you send a message through this website, we get your name, how to reach you, the language you prefer, the topic you chose and your message. The website emails it to our own inbox and keeps no copy. We use it only to answer you.

When you sign up as a member or volunteer, the website emails your sign-up to the same inbox in the same way. We also ask where you live and, for volunteers, what you can help with, so we can welcome you and ask you when something fits.

When you WhatsApp, call or email us, we see what you send us there, and we use it only to help you.

Our inbox is a Gmail address, so your messages are kept at Google. This website runs at Cloudflare. To show you the pages and keep the website safe, Cloudflare sees your internet address, as every website host does. The news and events we publish are stored inside the European Union.

## When you give

Gifts go through Stripe, a payment company, on Stripe's own payment page. You pay with iDEAL, through your own bank. A monthly gift is then collected every month by direct debit (SEPA).

In Stripe we see your name, your email address, the amount and the date, never your bank login. This website keeps nothing of your gift. With a monthly gift, Stripe keeps what it needs to collect it until you stop it. You can stop it at any time, yourself or by asking us. Stripe also has its own privacy statement.

When you give by bank transfer, our bank statement shows us your name, your account number and the amount.

## Cookies

This website sets no cookies for visitors: no tracking, no advertising and no statistics. That is why there is no cookie banner.

Your own browser remembers two small things, and only your browser: whether you chose light or dark, and that you closed an announcement. None of this is sent to us. Your language is part of the web address, so nothing needs to be stored for it.

We show no maps, videos or social media from other companies on our pages. A link such as Open in maps, WhatsApp or Stripe's payment page takes you to that company, which has its own rules and cookies.

Only the volunteers who sign in to edit this website get a sign-in cookie. Visitors never do.

## How long we keep it

Messages and sign-ups: as long as we need them to help you, or while you are a member. After that we delete them.

Gifts and other money matters: seven years, because Dutch law asks us to keep our financial records that long.

## Your rights

You may ask to see the details we have about you, to correct them or to delete them. You may also say no to us using them, or ask for a copy to take elsewhere. Write to us and we answer within a month.

If you think we handle your details badly, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the [Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/).
